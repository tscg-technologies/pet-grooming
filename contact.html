General Grooming Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about routine pet grooming visits. All guidance is general and cosmetic — always consult a veterinarian for medical or health concerns.

Grooming frequency depends on your pet's coat type, hair length, and daily lifestyle. Many curly or long-coated pets benefit from routine brushing at home alongside salon visits every 4 to 6 weeks, while short-coated pets may visit every 6 to 8 weeks for a refreshing bath, de-shedding brush-out, and nail trim.

Please bring your pet secured on a comfortable collar or harness and leash (or in a secure carrier for cats and small pets). You may also bring reference photos of your preferred coat style and let the groomer know about any sensitive areas or handling preferences.

Introductory puppy sessions focus on gentle familiarization — such as brief brushing, warm water introduction, and paw handling — so young dogs build positive associations. Suitability depends on the puppy's age, vaccination readiness as advised by your veterinarian, and individual temperament.

Yes! Owners are encouraged to share their preferred coat length and styling ideas before the session begins. The groomer will discuss how your preference works with your pet's coat texture, current tangles, and comfort needs.

Session duration varies based on your pet's size, coat density, temperament, and the requested services. A simple nail trim or basic bath is much quicker than a full bath, fluff dry, de-shedding, and full-body scissor styling session, especially when rest breaks are included for comfort.
